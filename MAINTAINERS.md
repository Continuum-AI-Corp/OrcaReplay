## Governance

Today this is maintainer-led: the people above merge changes and cut releases.

**The path out of that is written down on purpose.** Once three maintainers from outside the
founding organisation are active, we form a technical steering committee with a published charter,
and format changes require its consent rather than ours. Ambiguity about who controls an open
format is a reason not to build on it, so we would rather commit to the trigger now than be asked
about it later.

## Becoming a maintainer

Sustained, high-quality contribution in an area, plus the judgement to say no to changes that widen
the surface. Adapters and providers are the usual route in. Ask — we would rather have the
conversation early than have you guess.
